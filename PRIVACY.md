# Privacy policy

ChatGPT to Word/PDF processes conversation content locally in the browser only when the user starts an export.

- No account, subscription, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or remote conversion service is used.
- Conversation text is read from the open ChatGPT page and written directly into the requested local document.
- Images are fetched only when they appear in exported content. Requests go directly to the image host; supported OpenAI and Azure media hosts are declared in the manifest.
- No conversation content, identity, browsing history, or exported document is collected, sold, retained, or transmitted to the extension developer.
- The extension runs only on `chatgpt.com` and the legacy `chat.openai.com` domain.

The user can remove the extension at any time through the browser's extension settings.
